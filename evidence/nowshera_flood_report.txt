NEWS REPORT - Flash floods damage homes on Canal Road, Nowshera
Published: 20 September 2026

Nowshera, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa: District authorities confirmed on Sunday that flash flooding damaged residential properties along Canal Road, Nowshera. The property at 12 Canal Road was among those flooded, with water entering the ground floor and damaging furniture and electrical equipment.

A district rescue official said assessment teams visited 12 Canal Road and neighbouring houses and recorded flood damage to the ground floors. Photographs taken by the assessment team show standing water inside the property at 12 Canal Road.
